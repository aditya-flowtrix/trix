Utilities are for overriding one instance — a change or two that stand on
their own. When several have to hold together to make a look, that look is a
variant ([Utility or prop?](/docs/system/utilities-or-props)). They sit in the
last [cascade layer](/docs/system/the-cascade), so they always win.

This list is read from the stylesheets when the site builds.

## Generated from the tokens

Spacing, section spacing, radius, line height, letter spacing, weight and
grid spans follow the scales in `tokens.css`: add a step there and its
utilities appear here. `N` is the step.
