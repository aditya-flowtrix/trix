```astro live stage="stack"
  <Choice variant="chip" label="Design" />
  <Choice variant="chip" label="Code" checked />
</Fieldset>
```

`type="radio"` for one of several, a checkbox otherwise. Radios share a
`name`; a checkbox's `name` defaults to its label's slug. `variant="toggle"` is
a switch: a checkbox with the `switch` attribute and `role="switch"`, so screen
readers call it on or off. `required` marks the label, as on
[`Input`](/docs/components/input); a disabled `Choice` fades and shows it can't
be clicked. Each is at least 2.75rem tall, so it is easy to hit. Content inside
a `Choice` follows its label: [`FilterField`](/docs/components/filter) puts its
count there.
