`Nav` is the project's own: `BaseLayout` renders it on every page, and you
replace its links with the site's. On small screens the links fold into a menu
button; on large ones they sit in the bar.

```astro
<ul class="nav_links">
  <NavLink href="/">Home</NavLink>
  <NavLink href="/work">Work</NavLink>
  <NavLink href="/about">About</NavLink>
</ul>
```

`NavLink` marks itself with `aria-current="page"` when its `href` is the page
being shown — whatever the URL's trailing slash or `.html`.

`overlap` (on `BaseLayout`) fixes the bar over the first section instead of
above it — give that section `paddingTop="navoverlap"` to clear it.
