`Nav` is the project's own: `BaseLayout` renders it on every page, and you
replace its links with the site's. On small screens the links fold into a menu
button; on large ones they sit in the bar.

```astro
<ul class="nav_links">
  <NavLink href="/">Home</NavLink>
  <NavLink href="/work">Work</NavLink>
  <NavLink href="/about">About</NavLink>
</ul>
```

A group of links is a `Dropdown variant="menu"` in its own `<li>`, its links
a `nav_sub` list:

```astro
<li>
  <Dropdown variant="menu" label="Design system">
    <ul class="nav_sub">
      <NavLink href="/style-guide">Style Guide</NavLink>
      <NavLink href="/components">Components</NavLink>
    </ul>
  </Dropdown>
</li>
```

On small screens it opens inside the menu; on large ones it floats under the
bar, toward the middle of the page.

An announcement above the bar is a [`NavBanner`](/docs/components/nav-banner),
placed just before `<Nav />` in `BaseLayout`.

`NavLink` marks itself with `aria-current="page"` when its `href` is the page
being shown — whatever the URL's trailing slash or `.html`. A `#` link, a
placeholder or an anchor on the page, is never marked.

`overlap` (on `BaseLayout`) fixes the bar over the first section instead of
above it — give that section `paddingTop="navoverlap"` to clear it.
