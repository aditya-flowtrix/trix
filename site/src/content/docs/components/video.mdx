By default a video autoplays, muted and looping, at 16:9 — the background
video most sites use. `variant` sets the ratio: `2-1`, `16-9`, `3-2`, `5-4`,
`1-1`, `4-5`, `2-3`, `custom` (with `ratio`), or `auto` — the video's own
shape, which needs its `width` and `height` so the box holds its size before
the video loads.

## Loading and playback

An autoplaying video downloads none of the video until it scrolls into view,
plays while it is on screen and pauses when it leaves — a page of videos
costs only the ones being watched. Give it a `poster`: that is what shows
until then, and what shows without JavaScript.

`loading` is `lazy` by default: in browsers that lazy-load video (Chrome and
Edge) the poster and metadata wait until the video nears the screen too;
others fetch the poster up front. A video above the fold — a hero — takes
`loading="eager"`.

`desktopOnly` keeps a heavy background video off phones and tablets: below
the `large` breakpoint the poster stands alone and nothing downloads.

## The pause button

Moving content that plays for more than five seconds must be pausable (WCAG
2.2.2), so an autoplaying video shows a pause button. `pausePosition` puts it
in any of nine places:

```astro
<Video src="/video/demo.webm" pausePosition="top-left" />
```

`top-left`, `top`, `top-right`, `left`, `center`, `right`, `bottom-left`,
`bottom` and `bottom-right` (default). With reduced motion the video starts
paused, and the button is still there: reduced motion doesn't replace it, as
visitors who haven't turned the setting on need to stop the video too.
`pauseButton={false}` removes it — only for a video that stops within five
seconds and doesn't loop.

## Background video

A video behind a section's content goes in `Section`'s `background` slot,
where it fills the section. It keeps its pause button, which stays clickable
over the background:

```astro
<Section theme="dark">
  <Video slot="background" src="/video/demo.webm" poster="/video/demo.jpg" />
  <Heading>Built to last</Heading>
</Section>
```

## Captions

Anything inside `Video` goes inside the `<video>`, so a video with speech
takes its captions as a `<track>`:

```astro
<Video
  src="/video/tour.webm"
  poster="/video/tour.jpg"
  autoplay={false}
  muted={false}
  loop={false}
  controls
>
  <track
    kind="captions"
    src="/video/tour.en.vtt"
    srclang="en"
    label="English"
  />
</Video>
```
