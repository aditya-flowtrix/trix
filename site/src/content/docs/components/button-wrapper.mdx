---
title: ButtonWrapper
description: A row of buttons, links or tags — spaced, wrapping, and aligned with the block around it.
order: 14
component: wrapper/ButtonWrapper
---

```astro live stage="stack"
<ButtonWrapper marginTop="0">
  <Button>Primary</Button>
  <Button emphasis="secondary">Secondary</Button>
  <Button variant="link" element="link" href="/docs">Link</Button>
</ButtonWrapper>
```

It follows the alignment of the `ContentWrapper` or `Section` it sits in, and
brings its own space above (`marginTop`), so a button row under text needs no
margin of its own.
