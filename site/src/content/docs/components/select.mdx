```astro live stage="stack"
<Select label="Role" options={["Designer", "Developer", "Producer"]} />
<Select variant="state" />
```

`variant="list"` takes `options` — strings, or `{ label, value }`. `month`,
`day`, `year` (with `from` and `to`) and `state` bring their own. It is the
browser's own select, so it works with every keyboard, screen reader and
phone picker.
