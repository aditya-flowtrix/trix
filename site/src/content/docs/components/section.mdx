Every page is a stack of sections. A `Section` renders three things: the band
(`<section>`, with its theme and padding), an optional background behind
everything, and a container at the site's width whose children are spaced by
`gap`.

## Usage

```astro live stage="full"
<Section theme="dark" paddingTop="small" paddingBottom="small" gap="4">
  <Eyebrow>Eyebrow</Eyebrow>
  <Heading tag="h3" variant="h4">A section with a gap of 5</Heading>
  <Paragraph>Every block inside is spaced by the container's gap.</Paragraph>
</Section>
```

## Padding

`paddingTop` and `paddingBottom` take the section sizes — `xsmall` …
`xlarge`, fluid tokens — plus `none`, `even` and `navoverlap` (enough to clear
a nav fixed over the page). The default is `medium`.

## Background

Anything in the `background` slot fills the band behind the content: an image,
a video, an overlay.

```astro
<Section fullHeight theme="dark">
  <Img src={hero} slot="background" />
  <Overlay slot="background" />
  <Heading tag="h1">Over the image</Heading>
</Section>
```

## Alignment

`align="center"` centers the container's children and their text together.
