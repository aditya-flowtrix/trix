---
title: Paragraph
description: Body text in any text style, with a measure in characters so lines stay comfortable to read.
order: 21
component: typography/Paragraph
---

```astro live stage="stack"
<Paragraph variant="large" maxWidth={40}
  >Large text for an introduction, capped at forty characters a line.</Paragraph
>
<Paragraph maxWidth={60}
  >Main text — the default. A line of 60 to 75 characters is the most
  comfortable to read, which is what maxWidth is for.</Paragraph
>
<Paragraph variant="small">Small text for captions and notes.</Paragraph>
```

`variant` defaults to `inherit`, so a paragraph takes the style of what it
sits in. `tag` makes it a `span` or `div` where a `p` would be wrong.
