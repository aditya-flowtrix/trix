---
title: Overlay
description: A tint over an image or video, even or as a gradient, so the text on top of it stays readable.
order: 43
component: media/Overlay
---

```astro
<Section theme="dark" fullHeight>
  <Img src={hero} slot="background" />
  <Overlay slot="background" strength={60} />
  <Heading tag="h1">Readable over any image</Heading>
</Section>
```

The overlay tints toward the theme's background, so the theme's text reads on
top: it darkens in a dark section or card and lightens in a light one. A
section or card that inherits the page's theme follows light and dark mode
with it; `theme="dark"` keeps one dark in both. `solid` tints evenly;
`gradient` tints more toward the bottom, where the text usually sits.
`strength` is how strong, as a percentage.
