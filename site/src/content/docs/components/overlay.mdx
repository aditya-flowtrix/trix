---
title: Overlay
description: A tint over an image or video, even or as a gradient, so the text on top of it stays readable.
order: 43
component: media/Overlay
---

```astro
<Section theme="dark" fullHeight>
  <Img src={hero} slot="background" />
  <Overlay slot="background" strength={60} />
  <Heading tag="h1">Readable over any image</Heading>
</Section>
```

`solid` darkens evenly; `gradient` darkens more toward the bottom, where the
text usually sits. `strength` is how dark, as a percentage.
