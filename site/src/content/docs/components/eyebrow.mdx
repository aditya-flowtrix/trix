---
title: Eyebrow
description: The short label above a heading — with a marker, or as a tag — that leaves the spacing to the heading.
order: 22
component: typography/Eyebrow
---

```astro live stage="stack"
<Eyebrow>Services</Eyebrow>
<Eyebrow variant="tag">New</Eyebrow>
```

The space between an eyebrow and the heading under it belongs to the heading,
so an eyebrow carries no margin. `tag` makes it a heading element when the
label is part of the outline.
