```astro live stage="stack"
    <TabLink>Development</TabLink>

  <Paragraph>Design: how it looks and feels.</Paragraph>
  <Paragraph>Development: building it, and proving it works.</Paragraph>
</Tabs>
```

`TabLink`s go in the `links` slot; every other child is a panel, in the same
order. `label` names the tab list for screen readers — say what the tabs
choose between. Arrow keys move between tabs, Home and End to the first and
last.

The first tab is selected in the HTML, before any script runs. The other
panels are `hidden="until-found"`: find-in-page still finds their text and
selects their tab (Chrome and Firefox; Safari leaves hidden panels out of its
search). Each panel sits in a wrapper of its own, so padding, borders and
backgrounds stay on the panel and never show while it is hidden.

The selected tab is underlined — down the side, marked by a bar at its start —
as well as colored, and the mark is a border, which forced-colors mode keeps.
A row of tabs too long for its space scrolls, its hidden ends faded.

The selected tab is in the URL, so a link can open a page on the right panel:
`?tab=design` names the tab by its text, in any language, and a tab with no
text (an icon alone) by its position, `?tab=2`. Each Tabs on a page keeps its
own place: its `id`, or for those without one, `tab` for the first and then
`tab-2`, `tab-3`.

| Prop              | Does                                                                                                              |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `variant="side"`  | the tab list runs down the side from 48rem                                                                        |
| `playback="auto"` | advances on its own, with a pause control; holds while focused or off screen, and never moves with reduced motion |
