```astro live stage="stack"
<Tabs id="docs-tabs">
  <Fragment slot="links">
    <TabLink>Strategy</TabLink>
    <TabLink>Design</TabLink>
    <TabLink>Development</TabLink>
  </Fragment>
  <Paragraph>Strategy: what to build, and why.</Paragraph>
  <Paragraph>Design: how it looks and feels.</Paragraph>
  <Paragraph>Development: building it, and proving it works.</Paragraph>
</Tabs>
```

`TabLink`s go in the `links` slot; every other child is a panel, in the same
order. Arrow keys move between tabs, Home and End to the first and last.

The selected tab is in the URL, so a link can open a page on the right panel:
`?tab=design` names the tab by its text, in any language, and a tab with no
text (an icon alone) by its position, `?tab=2`. Each Tabs on a page keeps its
own place: its `id`, or for those without one, `tab` for the first and then
`tab-2`, `tab-3`.

| Prop              | Does                                                                                                              |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `variant="side"`  | the tab list runs down the side from 62rem                                                                        |
| `playback="auto"` | advances on its own, with a pause control; holds while focused or off screen, and never moves with reduced motion |
