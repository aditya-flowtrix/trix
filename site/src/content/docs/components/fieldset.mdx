---
title: Fieldset
description: A group of fields with its legend, laid out in a stack, columns, thirds or a row.
order: 61
component: form/Fieldset
---

```astro live stage="stack"
<Fieldset legend="Date of birth" variant="thirds" legendStyle="label">
  <Select variant="month" labelHidden />
  <Select variant="day" labelHidden />
  <Select variant="year" from={1940} labelHidden />
</Fieldset>
```

| Variant   | Layout                                 |
| --------- | -------------------------------------- |
| `stack`   | one field per line (default)           |
| `columns` | two columns from 48rem                 |
| `thirds`  | three columns from 48rem               |
| `inline`  | a wrapping row — radios and checkboxes |

`legendStyle` shows the legend as a heading, as a field label, or only to
screen readers. Fieldsets nest.
