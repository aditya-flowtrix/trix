```astro live stage="block"
  </div>

  </div>
</Compare>
```

`before` shows on the left of the divider and `after` on the right. They
share one space, so give them the same size — two images of one ratio, or two
blocks with the same `ratio-*` class. A label inside a side is cut with it.

The divider is a native range input: drag it, click anywhere along it, or
focus it and use the arrow keys. Without JavaScript it stays where `start`
puts it. A `handle` slot replaces the arrows in the knob.

Restyle the divider from a class on the component: `--_line-color`,
`--_line-width`, `--_knob-size`, `--_knob-background` and `--_knob-color`.
