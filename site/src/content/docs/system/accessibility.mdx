## Built in

- **The right element.** Links are `<a>`, buttons are `<button>`, disclosures
  are `<details>`, dialogs are `<dialog>`. Keyboard and screen-reader behaviour
  come from the browser, and work without JavaScript.
- **Skip link** to the main content, first in the page.
- **Visible focus** on everything focusable, from `--focus-width` and
  `--focus-offset`.
- **Text scales with the reader's font size** — every length is `rem`
  ([Fluid sizing](/docs/system/fluid-sizing)).
- **Contrast** — every text pair in every theme is measured against WCAG AA by
  `npm run check`.
- **Motion** — reveals, video and marquees respect reduced motion; moving
  content can be paused ([Motion](/docs/system/motion)).
- **Targets** — interactive controls are at least 24 by 24 px.
- **Headings** — `Heading`'s `tag` keeps the outline right whatever the size.
- **Images** — `Img` without `alt` is decorative (`alt=""`); with it, described.
- **Forms** — every field has a label (visible or not), `required` is marked,
  and inputs carry the right `type` and `autocomplete`.

## Checked

`npm run test:browsers` runs [axe](https://github.com/dequelabs/axe-core)
against WCAG 2.2 AA on every page, at phone, tablet and desktop widths, in
every installed browser — then drives the skip link, accordion, dropdown,
tabs, modal, slider, marquee and video with the keyboard. CI runs it in
Chrome, Edge, Brave, Firefox, WebKit and Safari on every push. See
[Testing](/docs/guides/testing).

Automated checks find about half of accessibility problems. The rest — reading
order that makes sense, alt text that says the right thing, a flow a screen
reader user can follow — still needs a person.
