## Reveal on scroll

`Section`, `Row`, `Col` and `Grid` take `animate`. Their children fade up one
after another the first time they scroll into view:

```astro
<Row animate>
  <Col span={4}>…</Col>
  <Col span={4}>…</Col>
  <Col span={4}>…</Col>
</Row>
```

Timing is four tokens: `--reveal-duration`, `--reveal-ease`,
`--reveal-distance` and `--reveal-stagger`. The script is the `Reveal`
component, which `BaseLayout` includes — a custom layout must include it too.

The content is never hidden from anyone who can't see it animate: visitors who
prefer reduced motion, printed pages, and browsers without JavaScript see it
straight away.

## Hover and open

`--hover-duration` and `--hover-ease` time every hover; `--open-duration` and
`--open-ease` every accordion, dropdown and menu. One change in `tokens.css`
retimes the site.

## Moving content can be paused

WCAG 2.2.2: anything that moves for more than five seconds must be pausable.

- **Video** that autoplays shows a pause button — `pauseButton`, on by default
  with `autoplay`, in any of nine `pausePosition`s.
- **Marquee** has a pause control, and holds still while anything inside it
  has focus, so a link can't travel away from the keyboard.
- **Tabs** with `playback="auto"` have a pause control, and hold while
  focused or off screen.

With reduced motion, autoplaying video starts paused, marquees stand still and
tabs don't advance.
